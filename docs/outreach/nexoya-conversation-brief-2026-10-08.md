## Conditional opening, only in a permitted conversation

“Your site describes collecting information before specialists take over, with ticket context and evidence already retained. Is there a recurring non-production check that still waits for the original engineer, or does the current setup handle that well?”

This is a question, not an assertion of pain. Do not turn it into an outbound campaign without the separate approval and contact-basis review.

## Questions that decide whether to continue

1. Can they describe one recent sanitized example? What was checked, by which role, and why?
2. How often does it recur? Separate active work from waiting; ask for reported or observed evidence, not guessed savings.
3. What already solves it: runbook, ticketing, CI, remote support or scripts? Why is a change needed, if at all?
4. Can another authorized operator perform it? Where exactly do they get stuck?
5. What confirms success? What happens on failure or an unclear outcome, and who owns investigation?
6. Who owns this workflow and can decide on a small paid assessment? Is any budget or willingness to pay actually expressed?
7. Are sanitized inputs permitted, and can Yash competently deliver the agreed review without credentials or server access?

Stop if their existing setup is adequate, the issue is non-recurring, no decision owner exists, inputs cannot be safely shared, or delivery capability is unconfirmed. Unknown answers stay unknown. No Axon installation is proposed by this brief.
