## First-hand discovery worksheet

Record answers, source/date, unknowns and any restrictions; do not replace missing answers with scores.

1. May we discuss the workflow, and may there be a follow-up through a specified channel? Who can grant that permission?
2. Show a sanitized recent occurrence, ideally within 90 days: request, environment, who needed help and what result was needed. Ask for a description, not access or secrets.
3. How often does the same task occur? Separate engineer active minutes, operator active minutes and elapsed waiting; waiting is not automatically billable effort.
4. Which teammate should perform it, and what exact authority should they have? What should remain forbidden?
5. What already exists: ticket portal, runbook, dashboards, CI, scripts or job tools? What was tried, and why does it still fail for this team?
6. Is the obstacle missing functionality, awareness, setup capacity, usability, maintenance or affordable support? Would a process/access change or existing-tool setup solve it?
7. Who owns the operational problem, approves spending and decides? Is there a budget and willingness to pay for a bounded assessment? Both remain unknown until answered.
8. What OS, network, data and customer-contract boundaries apply? Is a genuinely isolated staging example available, with authority from its owner?
9. What baseline should a second authorized operator beat? Agree observable completion, help required, active effort, forbidden effects and understandable uncertainty; set no invented savings target.
10. What installation/training/support work would be required, at what agreed hours and exclusions? Who maintains it, and what follow-up is permitted?
