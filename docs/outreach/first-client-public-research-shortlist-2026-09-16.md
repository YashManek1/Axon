# Axon first-client public research shortlist

Prepared 16 September 2026. Owner: Yash. This is a research artifact, not a contact list or send authorization.

No personal email, phone number, scraped profile, inferred address, credential, or sensitive personal data is included. The entries are organizations with public business pages. Company size, buyer identity, current procedure, pain, budget, jurisdiction, and permission for a safe assessment are unknown unless explicitly stated by the organization. Do not turn a website claim into a personalized claim without rechecking it immediately before any approved approach.

## How to use this shortlist

Use the official business page as the provenance record, then verify a general company route and the recipient’s role through that route. Ask for a founder/CTO/infrastructure or delivery lead only when the organization confirms that role. Do not guess an email pattern, buy data, scrape a directory, or contact an employee through a second channel after an unanswered first touch.

The score below uses the 10-point account score in the main plan. It is intentionally conservative: a public description of services shows a possible fit, not a proven pain or a safe target. None of these entries is approved for contact. A cold approach requires a current score of at least 7/10, no exclusion, a verified business route, a human-reviewed claim ledger, and the pre-send checklist.
