# Axon organization research shortlist — 2026-09-16

## Purpose and evidence rule

This is an organization-level research list, not a send list. It contains no person-level contact data. Facts below come from each organization's own public site and are labelled **verified**. **Inference / gap** is an Axon research hypothesis, not a claim about the organization.

The existing 10-point rubric is applied without softening: repeated procedure (R), measurable cost/failure pain (P), buyer access/budget (B), alternative gap (A), and safe assessment target (S), each 0–2. Cold outreach requires **7/10 and no exclusion**. A score of 6 is eligible for warm discovery only when a real referrer exists. Public service copy proves capability; it does not prove that the provider has the same unsolved internal pain.

### 1. DailyDeploy — 6/10 (R1 P1 B2 A1 S1)

- **Verified:** Its site describes a founder-led boutique consultancy for cloud, Kubernetes, CI/CD, IaC, observability and operational runbooks. It publishes prior client outcomes such as deploy time reductions and CI cost savings, and says engagements range from a two-week audit to multi-month builds. ([first-party site](https://dailydeploy.in/))
- **Inference / evidence burden:** A founder-operated consultancy likely repeats delivery and handoff work, but the case-study numbers describe client outcomes. They do not establish DailyDeploy's own present execution cost, failure rate, or workaround.
- **Blocking fact:** one current, first-party example of DailyDeploy's own repeated internal runbook with measured time, rerun, incident, or reviewer-wait cost.
- **Research prompt:** “Does your own delivery team repeat any non-production infrastructure validation or handoff runbook across client environments, and what does one run cost in engineer time or rework?”
- **Discovery hypothesis:** a synthetic pre-handoff validation workflow could reduce founder review time without touching client production.
