### What you receive

1. A 60-minute review of one recent, real procedure: its purpose, responsible people, intended environment, steps, current tools and recorded result.

3. A one-page recommendation: keep the current approach, improve its runbook/tooling, or consider a separately scoped implementation. Include the next concrete action, responsible role, and a way to check whether the improvement helped.

This is advisory work, not software installation. No production changes, credentials, remote execution, guaranteed savings, ongoing support or Axon subscription are included. A demonstration is not required for delivery and is excluded unless separately authorized and confirmed achievable.
